What this run revealed

Four observations worth carrying into the next pass.

Geography

90% non-US, 24 countries

Only three of the thirty products are based in the US. Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Romania, Ukraine, Taiwan and Bulgaria all show up — samples almost no English-language indie roundup covers.

Data quality

Zero missing fields

Every product carries a verified pricing URL and at least one growth-signal source link. No field falls back to “data not found” — the price of saying no when verification fails.

Category balance

Not just AI tools

Fewer than a third of the picks depend on an LLM. Invoicing, clinic management, PDF utilities and resume builders sit alongside AI video clippers — closer to the real long tail of indie revenue.

Blind spots

LATAM, MENA, Africa, China

Beyond Brazil and Israel, the next run needs deliberate searches in Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic, plus Chinese-language indie communities. Entire regions never surfaced this time.
